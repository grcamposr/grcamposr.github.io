Wecide

Wecide helps a group of people pick a film to watch together. There is no sign-up, no email address and no password.

How It Works

  1. Start a Room: An anonymous identifier is created automatically the first time you open the app to tell your device apart from others.
  2. Invite Friends: Users join a shared session using a room code.
  3. Set the Vibe: The room settings (streaming services, moods, eras, session length, and country) choose which films appear in the deck.
  4. Vote: Submit your vote on each film: like, pass, or super like.
  5. Decide: Detects when everyone agrees, and builds the results screen at the end.

Privacy & Design

Wecide shows no advertising and contains no analytics or crash-reporting software. Everything is built around simplicity and ephemerality.

Rooms and all associated room data are deleted automatically 24 hours after creation. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.
