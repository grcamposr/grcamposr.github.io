<section class="card">
  <h2>How It Works</h2>
  <ol>
    <li><strong>Start a Room:</strong> An anonymous identifier is created automatically the first time you open the app to tell your device apart from others.</li>
    <li><strong>Invite Friends:</strong> Users join a shared session using a room code.</li>
    <li><strong>Set the Vibe:</strong> The room settings (streaming services, moods, eras, session length, and country) choose which films appear in the deck.</li>
    <li><strong>Vote:</strong> Submit your vote on each film: like, pass, or super like.</li>
    <li><strong>Decide:</strong> Detects when everyone agrees, and builds the results screen at the end.</li>
  </ol>
</section>
