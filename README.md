# Wecide-public
🎬 STOP ARGUING. START WATCHING.

Can't decide what to watch tonight?

Wecide is the movie night app that helps friends, couples and groups choose a movie everyone actually wants to watch.

Create a room, invite your friends with a simple code, and swipe through the same movies together. Like a movie, pass on it, or use your one Super Like. When everyone likes the same film, you've got a match.

No endless scrolling. No “you pick.” No arguing over what movie to watch.

🍿 HOW WECIDE WORKS

1. Create a movie night room
2. Share the 4-digit room code with your friends
3. Choose your streaming services, mood, era and session length
4. Everyone swipes through the same movie deck
5. When everyone likes a movie, Wecide finds your match
6. See the movie details and where it's available to stream

👥 PICK A MOVIE TOGETHER

Wecide is built for the actual decision: finding a movie that the whole group wants to watch.

Whether it's a date night, movie night with friends, a family evening or a group hangout, everyone gets a vote.

Up to 8 people can join a room, so you can make the decision together instead of passing the remote around.

❤️ SWIPE. LIKE. MATCH.

Swipe left to pass.
Swipe right to like.
Swipe up for a Super Like.

Your Super Like highlights the movie you're really hoping everyone chooses.

The first movie liked by everyone becomes your group match.

📺 FIND MOVIES YOU CAN ACTUALLY WATCH

Filter your movie night by the streaming services available to you, along with moods, genres and eras.

Wecide focuses on movies available through subscription, free or ad-supported streaming options, so you can spend less time searching across streaming apps and more time watching.

⏱️ A MOVIE PICKER WITH A TIMER

Don't let choosing a movie take longer than watching one.

Choose a 5, 10 or 15-minute session and Wecide builds a movie deck for your group. Everyone swipes until you find a match—or until the session ends.

🏆 NO MATCH? YOU STILL GET AN ANSWER.

If nobody finds a unanimous match, Wecide shows the near misses so your group can make a decision from the results.

See:
• Movies everyone liked
• Movies almost everyone liked
• Super Likes
• Each person's tally

So even when everyone doesn't agree, movie night doesn't have to start over.

No account. No password. No social login.

Enter your name, join the room with a 4-digit code, and you're ready to pick a movie.
