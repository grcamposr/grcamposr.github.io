Privacy Policy

Wecide helps a group of people pick a film to watch together. There is no sign-up, no email address and no password.

Last updated: 1 September 2026

About Wecide

Wecide is published and operated by Carolina Campos, who is responsible for the information described in this policy.

If you have a question about this policy, email gr.camposr@gmail.com.

What the app stores about you

Everything in this table lives in our database, which is hosted by Supabase.

Information Why it exists Who can see it
The display name you type It labels your votes so the room knows who liked what Everyone in your room
An anonymous identifier Created automatically the first time you open the app. It tells your device apart from the other devices in a room. It is tied to the display name you type and to nothing else about you Nobody; it is used internally
The room code and who is in the room Puts you and your friends into the same session Everyone in your room
Your vote on each film: like, pass or super like Detects when everyone agrees, and builds the results screen at the end Everyone in your room, on the results screen
The room's settings: streaming services, moods, eras, session length and the country your phone is set to Chooses which films appear in the deck Everyone in your room

What is sent to TMDB

The room settings are the only part that leaves our database. They are sent to TMDB as search parameters when the film list is built.

Your name, your anonymous identifier and your votes are never sent to TMDB or anywhere else.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location or advertising identifier.

Wecide shows no advertising and contains no analytics or crash-reporting software.

What the app stores on your phone

The app keeps a small amount of data on the device itself so that closing and reopening it does not lose your place:

  • Your display name
  • The room you are currently in together with its film list
  • The sign-in session for your anonymous identifier
  • A clock offset used for the countdown

This data never leaves your phone by itself, and Android's automatic backup is switched off for Wecide, so it is not copied to your Google account. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase hosts our database and issues the anonymous sign-in. It processes the information in the table above on our behalf. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • TMDB supplies the film information, posters and streaming availability. It receives the room settings as search parameters, and your phone loads posters directly from its servers, so TMDB sees your device's network address while you swipe. It never receives your name, your identifier or your votes. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. See TMDB's privacy policy.
  • JustWatch produces the streaming availability data. We send JustWatch nothing; the data reaches us through TMDB.

Like any internet service, the servers the app connects to record your device's network address in their connection logs.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone beyond what this section describes.

How long it lasts

Nothing is stored long term. A room and everything in it—the members, the votes, the settings and the code—is deleted automatically 24 hours after the room was created.

Anonymous identifiers that no longer belong to any room are deleted in the same sweep. After that, nothing about your session remains on our side.

Children

Wecide is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

There is no account to delete, because the app never creates one, and there is no deletion request to make, because everything is deleted automatically within 24 hours.

Uninstalling Wecide removes what the app kept on your phone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it.
